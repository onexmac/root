## Team composition `[V]` — but contested

**Sources disagree on the third slot. See `perspectives.md` §1 before treating any of this as
settled.**

Agreed across all sources: **every winning comp contains at least one Medium and at least one
Heavy.** Both are S-tier. Nobody credible recommends stacking Light.

| Comp | Claim |
|---|---|
| **MHH** — Medium + 2 Heavy | "The dominant competitive setup; every tournament team keeps rediscovering it" `[V]` |
| **HML** — one of each | "Safest for most ranked players, especially solo queue" `[V]` |
| **LMH played well** | Highest ceiling — glitch grenades counter the other meta comps, Light works off-angles — but hardest to run, and struggles into MHH `[V]` |

`[I]` Reading the S-tier builds together, the intended role structure is legible: **Medium is the
economic engine** (Healing Beam sustains, Defibrillator converts deaths into saved coins),
**Heavy is the space-taker and displacer** (Winch Claw, Dome Shield, C4), **Light is the tempo and
information piece** (Dash, Gateway, Vanishing Bomb).

**Ranked implication `[I]`:** the Defibrillator sits in the S-tier Medium build, and given that a
respawn coin is spent on *respawn* rather than revive `[V]`, a Medium running defib is not just a
sustain pick — it is the team's primary coin-conservation tool. In ranked Cashout, a comp with no
defib is structurally leaking resources.

## Competitive scene `[V]`

Worth knowing where to look for authoritative doctrine.

- **Grand Major 2026** — Embark's flagship event, Stockholm, alongside DreamHack Stockholm.
  $150,000 prize pool. Functions as the World Championship.
- **Regional Fame Index (RFI)** — the 2026 global ranking system. Teams earn points by placement at
  sanctioned events, across three regions: EMEA, AMERICAS, APAC.
- **Two tiers:** Tier 1 is the Grand Major (year-end); Tier 2 is "Prestige Events" — sanctioned and
  community-run tournaments awarding RFI points.
- **Organisations competing:** M80, Pioneers, ENVY, Team Secret, Fnatic, Dragon Ranger Gaming,
  Vanguard Gaming, Alliance.

## The SR-84 question

The Commander's main. Honest read from current sources:

- The SR-84 **received buffs in Season 11 and moved up a tier.** `[V]`
- It is **not** in any S-tier list. `[V]`
- The recurring criticism: high damage, no mobility — the trade Light is least suited to make, since
  Light's 150 HP `[V]` is priced on the assumption you are using mobility to avoid damage.
- It genuinely excels at long range, and the 2× headshot multiplier rewards precision. `[V]`
- **It cannot one-shot a Medium or Heavy** on the S9 damage figures. `[I]` — see `numbers.md`, and
  note the unresolved conflict there.

`[I]` This does not mean "stop using it." It means the sniper's value is in the roles this substrate
already identifies as high-value: **objective denial at range, pressure on box carriers, and
information.** It is a poor tool for winning the fight at a cashout station and a good one for
making the enemy's approach to it expensive. Play it as a macro instrument, not a duelling weapon.
