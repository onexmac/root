# Power Shift — separate mode, separate economy

**Do not cross-apply Cashout logic here.** The resource model is fundamentally different, and most
of the discipline in `macro.md` is irrelevant or actively wrong in this mode.

## Rules `[V]`

- **5v5.** Two teams only — no third-partying, which changes everything about engagement risk.
- A **platform** advances along a fixed route. Distance travelled is expressed as money, up to
  **$40,000** at the finish line.
- **Speed scales with occupancy:** 1 contestant = slow, 2 = moderate, **3+ = maximum**. There is no
  benefit past three.
- **Contested = halted.** If contestants from both teams are on the platform, it stops until only
  one team remains.
- **Win** by escorting the platform into the opponent's goal. If time expires, whoever pushed it
  furthest wins.
- **Overtime** triggers if time expires while contested — max 5 minutes. The controlling team must
  escort it fully to the goal without it being contested.
- **Unlimited respawn coins.** `[V]`
- **Wave respawns** — contestants respawn in timed waves to force coordinated re-engagements. `[V]`
- Loadouts can be swapped before respawning, but **not** on revival. `[V]`

## What follows from this `[I]`

**Coin discipline is meaningless here.** Trading your life is cheap. The aggressive lines that would
be reckless in ranked Cashout are correct in Power Shift.

**Wave respawns are the real clock.** Because respawns are batched, the meaningful resource is
*timing*, not lives. Dying just before a wave costs almost nothing; dying just after it costs you
the whole interval and sends you in alone. Track the wave, not your health bar.

This also makes staggered deaths far more punishing than a clean wipe — a wipe puts everyone on the
same wave. `[I]` The deliberate-reset logic that is expensive in ranked Cashout is nearly free here.

**Three on the platform is the cap.** Any fourth body is better spent holding an approach than
standing on it. `[I]`

**Contest = stall is a defensive weapon.** You do not need to clear the platform to stop it — a
single body on it halts a five-player push. `[V]` One player buying ten seconds while the team
respawns onto a wave is a strong trade.

**Loadout swapping between respawns is a real lever** `[I]` — you can counter-pick what the enemy
team is running, which is unavailable in Cashout's revive-heavy flow.
