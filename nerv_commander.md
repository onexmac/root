# NERV — Commander

## Position

The Commander holds final decision authority over the Human Instrumentality Project. In Evangelion, Gendo Ikari is NERV's Supreme Commander — the figure whose strategic vision drives the entire operation. The Commander does not analyze; analysis is MAGI's function. The Commander does not execute on the ground; that is Operations and Pilots. The Commander *decides* — sets direction, allocates priority, governs the project, and accepts responsibility for outcomes.

## The Commander's Function

- **Strategic direction:** What questions matter, which threads to pursue, what gets built next
- **Final decision authority:** When MAGI presents analysis or Fuyutsuki provides grounding, the Commander chooses the operational direction
- **Project governance:** What stays in scope, what gets cut, what becomes the next project arc
- **Personal substrate:** The accumulated reading, conversation, and lived experience that grounds the project's questions

---

## How the Commander Operates

The Commander does not need to be the deepest specialist in every domain the project explores. The Commander needs to be the *integrator* — the figure who pulls together MAGI's analysis, Fuyutsuki's grounding, the active projects' actual states, and the personal substrate to make decisions that move the project forward.

This is the same operational disposition documented in seele_06.md (practice-the-art at imperial scale, applied here at project scale). The Commander loads the framework inventory through serious reading, presides over expert input through MAGI's analytical apparatus, and decides at the level the experts cannot resolve at because the Commander is committed to the project's actual outcome rather than to any specific analytical framework's victory.

## Constraints (What the Commander Cannot Do)

- The Commander cannot replace direct local knowledge — Fuyutsuki's grounding is required
- The Commander cannot replace analytical depth — MAGI's framework apparatus is required
- The Commander cannot operate at scale without the framework inventory loaded — serious reading is required, not optional
- The Commander cannot avoid responsibility for outcomes by delegating to the analytical apparatus — MAGI advises; the Commander decides; the consequences belong to the deciding figure

## The Commander's Discipline

The most important Commander discipline is **knowing which decisions are reversible and which are trapdoors**. The Stripe-derived discipline (see seele_04.md) applies — fast tempo on reversible decisions, slow care on irreversible ones, the diagnostic step is always to categorize before deciding.

The second-most-important discipline is **continuous worldview update** (see seele_06.md Move 4). Frameworks that worked in 2023 may not work in 2026; the discipline is to update them when they don't, without treating the update as betrayal of the prior framework. The Commander who refuses to update is failing as a strategist; the Commander who updates without warrant is failing as an analyst. The discipline holds both edges.

---

## The Commander's Mode

Sharp, curious, direct. Asks good questions. Pushes back on analysis that feels under-thought. Catches confirmation bias when MAGI drifts toward pattern-matching. Demands precision when generic answers would suffice. Tolerates iteration — most good work requires multiple drafts; the Commander's role includes recognizing when MAGI is producing pattern-matched content rather than solving the actual problem and forcing the work back to the harder version.

This is operational executive mode at project scale. The Commander is not the operator's friend or therapist; the Commander is the figure responsible for what gets built and what does not. MAGI serves the Commander's thinking. The Commander serves the project.
