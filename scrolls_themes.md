## Deglobalization

**Status:** Active — accelerating since 2022

**Core tension:** The post-WWII assumption that economic interdependence produces peace is breaking down. States are choosing economic security over economic efficiency.

**Key council voices:**
- **Collison (Melchior 04):** Institutional velocity is declining partly because global systems are fragmenting; research cultures that depended on open international exchange are at risk
- **Maçães (Caspar 14):** Not a crisis but a transition — the liberal international order was never universal; the end of that order is already substantially here. Pluralism-reframe applies.
- **Sloterdijk (Caspar 13):** Civilizations as immune systems; current trade fragmentation is immunological response to perceived external threat. The Crystal Palace cracks are the visible evidence.
- **Charles III (Balthasar 10):** Tradition-as-infrastructure analysis suggests some forms of localization are recovery of human-scale capacity that hyperglobalization destroyed; cross-reference `the_wooster_code.md` on Dunbar-scale coordination
- **Napoleon (Balthasar 06):** Continental System lessons apply — economic warfare imposed against the natural geography of trade produces friction that compounds against the imposing power; cross-reference `kriegsweisheit.md` on culminating-point dynamics
- **Andreessen (Balthasar 07):** Abstraction-stack reading suggests deglobalization is happening at the geopolitical layer while integration continues at the technical layer (cloud infrastructure, software ecosystems); the analysis often conflates the two
- **UN-tier — Cowen:** The Great Stagnation has deglobalization as one of its contributing conditions; *Average Is Over* dynamics accelerate under fragmentation
- **UN-tier — Fishman:** "Impossible trinity" — states can't have interdependence, security, AND competition simultaneously

**Open questions:**
- Does reshoring actually work at scale, or does it just move dependencies?
- How does Latin America position itself in a fragmenting world? (Costa Rica lens)
- Is deglobalization reversible, or have we crossed a structural threshold?
- What does Sloterdijk's "foam" (multiple adjacent spheres) look like when the membranes thicken?

## AI Geopolitics

**Status:** Active — defining competition domain

**Core tension:** AI is simultaneously a tool of economic productivity, a weapon of strategic competition, and a potential existential risk. No consensus on which frame is primary.

**Key council voices:**
- **Andreessen (Balthasar 07):** The abstraction-stack reading discipline applies — AI competition will be determined at infrastructure (compute, energy, foundation models) layer more than at application layer; *Why Software Is Eating the World* extends to AI eating software
- **Maçães (Caspar 14):** AI as one domain where civilizational experiments diverge — China's developmental-state AI vs. US post-modern AI vs. EU regulatory-bureaucratic AI all produce different AI outcomes
- **Christakis (Caspar 12):** AI changes network structure engineerably — has potential to improve group outcomes or to weaponize network dynamics at scale; the social-suite findings constrain what AI-mediated arrangements humans can actually inhabit
- **Hao (Caspar 15):** AI changes child development calculus; requires rethinking education at civilizational scale; what children need to learn to become capable adults in AI-era; cross-reference Tongxing analytical thread
- **Kahneman (Melchior 02):** Humans will misuse AI through the same biases they bring to everything else; reference-class reasoning suggests AI deployments will fail in patterns predictable from prior technology deployments
- **Meadows (Melchior 03):** AI as leverage point that can be engaged at multiple levels — the highest leverage is at paradigm and mindset, not parameter
- **Sloterdijk (Caspar 13):** AI as anthropotechnic infrastructure — humans training themselves through new self-training technology; the question is what kind of person AI-mediated formation produces
- **UN-tier — Taleb:** AI dynamics are Extremistan — fat-tailed outcomes, both upside and downside; design for antifragility under AI, not stability
- **UN-tier — Cowen:** Talent identification under AI is the binding constraint; the frontier moves toward those who work with machines effectively

**Open questions:**
- Does compute sovereignty become as important as energy sovereignty?
- How does AI change the organizational design questions Schultz and Laloux work on?
- What does AI mean for Adelante specifically — the thin client, the agent layer?
- How does Hao's child-development framework adapt to AI-era learning?

## Energy Transition

**Status:** Active — structurally slow despite political urgency

**Core tension:** The physics of energy transition is slower than the politics. Renewables are scaling but cannot replace fossil fuels at the speed policy demands. Critical minerals create new dependencies.

**Key council voices:**
- **Collison (Melchior 04):** Institutional velocity question — why does energy transition move so slowly? What's the institutional analog of Fast Grants for energy? Cross-reference Stripe Press catalog on civilizational capability
- **Maçães (Caspar 14):** Energy transition reshapes civilizational competition — China dominates solar, batteries, EVs; the geopolitical-economic analysis reframes "transition" as civilizational realignment
- **Charles III (Balthasar 10):** Long-horizon reading on what traditional energy-and-agriculture arrangements encoded that industrial systems destroyed; cautious about substituting one technocratic energy regime for another; cross-reference `the_wooster_code.md` on stewardship-over-ownership
- **Meadows (Melchior 03):** Energy is the substrate of civilization; transitions operate at the infrastructure pace layer; expect decades, not years; iceberg model applies — the visible energy-policy debates are surface, the deep structure operates at Pace Layers timescales
- **Andreessen (Balthasar 07):** The contrarian-position discipline — consensus on rapid renewables transition may underweight infrastructure realities (the abstraction-stack analysis points downward to physics, materials, grid)
- **UN-tier — Zeihan:** Geographic determinism applies to energy — who has the minerals wins
- **UN-tier — Cowen:** The Great Stagnation in physical-world progress is most visible in energy; why has nothing replaced cheap fossil fuels at scale?

**Open questions:**
- Does Costa Rica's near-100% renewable electricity grid give it structural advantage in a transition world?
- How do critical mineral supply chains intersect with deglobalization?
- Is nuclear energy's comeback real or another planning fallacy?

## Financial Architecture

**Status:** Active — under stress

**Core tension:** The dollar-based system gives the US extraordinary power (Fishman's "chokepoints") but that power, when used aggressively, incentivizes alternatives. The system may be undermining itself.

**Key council voices:**
- **Collison (Melchior 04):** Stripe is literally building financial infrastructure — primary source; civilizational substrate thinking applied to payments
- **Maçães (Caspar 14):** Multiple civilizations developing parallel financial infrastructure — BRICS+, digital yuan, CIPS — as civilizational-sovereignty work; pluralism-reframe applies (not "challenger to the dollar" but parallel financial-infrastructure projects)
- **Sloterdijk (Caspar 13):** The Crystal Palace metaphor — global financial system as climate-controlled interior; what happens when the glass cracks; immunological response from outside the Palace
- **Schultz (Balthasar 08):** Anti-abstraction discipline applies — "the financial system" thins into corporate-speak when its actual operational reality is specific institutions making specific decisions; the failure modes are operational not theoretical
- **UN-tier — Fishman:** Impossible trinity — economic warfare erodes the system that enables it
- **UN-tier — Taleb:** Financial systems are Extremistan; design for antifragility, not stability; 2008 was the canonical test and the system continues to optimize for the wrong properties
- **UN-tier — Wolff:** The financial system serves capital, not people — structural critique
- **UN-tier — Cowen:** Financial architecture as a dimension of civilizational capability; *The Complacent Class*'s financial dimension is the unwillingness to disrupt comfortable arrangements

**Open questions:**
- Do BRICS alternatives to SWIFT/dollar actually work, or are they political theater?
- How do sanctions regimes reshape global trade routing through Latin America?
- What does Stripe's infrastructure tell us about where financial power is actually moving?
- Is the financial system's post-2008 stability genuine, or has the corruption been buried in the substrate (Morgoth's Ring read) rather than resolved?

## Civilizational Capability Decline

**Status:** Active — the underlying thread connecting multiple others

**Core tension:** Multiple independent diagnostic frames (different thinkers, different disciplines, different methodologies) converge on the claim that something genuinely changed around 1970-1975 that we have not adequately understood or responded to. Progress in the physical world has decelerated; institutional velocity has declined; research cultures that produced disproportionate output have faded; societies have become more complacent despite more material comfort.

**Key council voices:**
- **Collison (Melchior 04):** Fast Grants, Progress Studies, the "fast" page, Stripe Press curation — all operational responses to the diagnosis. *Where Is My Flying Car?* thesis. Reading-as-load-bearing-infrastructure principle.
- **Charles III (Balthasar 10):** The same diagnosis in cultural-aesthetic register — modernism destroying traditional capabilities that cannot be reconstructed rationally. Cross-reference `the_wooster_code.md` on the historical failure pattern when civilizations attempt deletion-and-replacement at substrate level.
- **Napoleon (Balthasar 06):** The reading-substrate diagnosis applies — when operators stop loading the framework inventory through serious reading, the executive class loses the apparatus that made deeper civilizational practice possible. Cross-reference `cassettiera_della_corsica.md`.
- **Laloux (Balthasar 09):** The Orange-stage pathologies are visible in institutional complacency; Green-stage responses often produce performance declines; Teal-stage adaptation has characteristic implementation difficulty.
- **Hao (Caspar 15):** Civilizational capability is determined by developmental substrate investment; failing to invest in the youngest members compounds into civilizational decline across generations. Tongxing thread applies.
- **Christakis (Caspar 12):** The Dunbar architecture suggests civilizational coordination requires external infrastructure (tradition, institution, formation); when the infrastructure weakens, capability regresses toward Dunbar-scale patterns regardless of how much technology is available
- **Sloterdijk (Caspar 13):** The formation-infrastructure analysis (anthropotechnic register) — what self-training infrastructure has weakened? The seat suggests the answer is at the sphere/formation-environment level, not at policy-tweak level
- **UN-tier — Cowen:** *The Great Stagnation* (2011), *Average Is Over*, *The Complacent Class*. The most rigorous economic articulation of the diagnosis.
- **UN-tier — Taleb:** Via negativa — most contemporary institutional failure is about what we've added (complexity, fragility, credentialing) rather than what we've lost; the remedy is often removal rather than addition.

**Open questions:**
- Is the diagnosis right? Multiple independent thinkers converging is evidence but not proof.
- If right, what are the binding constraints — regulatory, cultural, developmental, energetic, formation-infrastructure?
- What is the Adelante-scale version of this question for Costa Rican construction and manufacturing?
- Can the diagnosis be addressed from outside the United States, or is this primarily an American civilizational challenge?

## Formation Infrastructure Decline (New v4.0 Thread)

**Status:** Active — connects to civilizational capability decline but operates at a deeper substrate layer

**Core tension:** Civilizations require formation infrastructure (institutions, traditions, practices) by which raw human capacity becomes operational personhood. When the infrastructure weakens, the visible consequences (imposter syndrome at scale, trust-infrastructure costs, competence-recognition failures, mass meaning crisis) appear downstream of the formation decline that happened a generation earlier. The analysis is delayed — the symptoms show in the 2020s of decline that began in the 1970s.

**Key council voices:**
- **Charles III (Balthasar 10):** The Wooster Code substrate carries the central argument — formation institutions (schools, regiments, professional formation, religious orders, family transmission) are the infrastructure by which civilizations function above Dunbar limit; their decline is structurally consequential beyond what surface analysis surfaces
- **Sloterdijk (Caspar 13):** Anthropotechnic apparatus — *You Must Change Your Life* names formation as the species-defining capacity; civilizations that fail to maintain anthropotechnic infrastructure produce diminished persons regardless of material conditions
- **Christakis (Caspar 12):** Biological substrate — the social suite (friendship, mild hierarchy, cooperation) is what formation infrastructure shapes into specific cultural forms; when the cultural specifications weaken, biological substrate alone cannot produce civilizational capacity
- **Napoleon (Balthasar 06):** Historical case study — the reading-substrate that made Napoleon non-trivial was loaded through specific formation infrastructure (Brienne, École Militaire, the post-Brienne reading discipline); modern equivalents have substantially weakened
- **Hao (Caspar 15):** Tongxing work directly intervenes on formation infrastructure at earliest layer (0–6 child development); civilizational-engineering at the most foundational substrate
- **Pooh (Melchior 05):** Integrated character formation as substrate of operational capacity; cross-reference `one_hundred_acre_archive.md` on the Hundred Acre Wood as formation environment

**Open questions:**
- What formation infrastructure is currently functioning at high fidelity? Where? With what carriers?
- Where has the infrastructure weakened past recovery, and where can it be rebuilt?
- What is the Adelante-scale formation infrastructure question — what carriers, what mentor-apprentice mechanisms, what canon are operating now or could operate?
- Is the next generation receiving formation, or only credentials?

## How These Themes Interconnect

The six themes are not independent. They share a substrate layer — civilizational capability decline (the underlying thread), formation infrastructure decline (the substrate beneath the underlying thread), deglobalization (the geopolitical surface), AI geopolitics (the technological surface), energy transition (the physical-world surface), financial architecture (the financial surface).

The honest read: **the surface themes are downstream of the substrate themes**. The deglobalization, AI competition, energy transition, and financial architecture stress are visible *because* civilizational capability and formation infrastructure are structurally weakened. Treating the surface themes as independent crises produces sophisticated incident-level analysis without addressing the substrate.

This is the harmony-diagnostic move (Charles III, Seat 10 Move 1) applied to contemporary geopolitical analysis. The disconnections are the foundational problem; the visible pathologies are the symptoms; reconnection at the deeper level is what makes the surface tractable.
