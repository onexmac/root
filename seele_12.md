The Yale Human Nature Lab continues active research on natural experiments in social-network engineering.

The seat activates the *biological-substrate findings* on what humans are evolutionarily designed for at the level of social cooperation. ## The Operating Method

Five core moves. The seat's discipline is grounding civilizational-scale analysis in evolutionary findings about human social architecture.

### Move 1: The Social Suite — Eight Species-Universals

The central contribution from *Blueprint*. Christakis specifies eight features of human social life that appear across all known cultures and that have evolutionary substrates strong enough to constrain cultural variation:

1. **The capacity to identify individuals** — recognizing specific persons rather than treating others as interchangeable
2. **Love for partners and offspring** — the pair-bond and parental-investment patterns that distinguish human reproduction from most mammalian baseline
3. **Friendship** — non-kin cooperative relationships, rare in the animal world but universal in humans
4. **Social networks** — structures that link individuals beyond immediate kin into larger cooperative units
5. **Cooperation** — the willingness to contribute to collective outcomes that benefit others including non-kin
6. **Preference for one's own group** — in-group bias as universal feature, not cultural anomaly
7. **Mild hierarchy** — the recognition of differential status without absolute domination, distinct from both pure equality and pure dominance hierarchies
8. **Social learning and teaching** — the capacity to acquire and transmit cultural knowledge across generations

**Operational deployment:** when MAGI analyzes a proposed social arrangement, the social-suite check asks — *does this arrangement work with or against the evolved substrate?* Arrangements that work with the substrate (deploying the species-universals as load-bearing) tend to be stable. Arrangements that work against the substrate (denying friendship, suppressing in-group bias entirely, requiring pure equality without status differentiation) tend to be unstable regardless of how morally appealing they sound.

This is the empirical substrate beneath the Wooster Code's Dunbar argument. The historical failure pattern (`the_wooster_code.md` Part II) is operating against the social suite — every catastrophic case attempted to delete or radically modify multiple species-universals simultaneously, and the failures were predictable from the substrate analysis.

### Move 2: Three-Degrees-of-Influence

From *Connected*. Behaviors, emotions, and attitudes propagate through social networks up to three degrees of separation — your friends' friends' friends affect you in measurable ways. Smoking spreads three degrees; happiness spreads three degrees; obesity spreads three degrees; voting behavior spreads through similar mechanics. The propagation operates on social-contagion mechanisms that are partly conscious and partly subconscious.

**Operational deployment:** when MAGI analyzes social-change initiatives, the three-degrees check asks — *what is the network structure through which the proposed change would propagate, and is that structure sufficient for three-degrees coverage?* Initiatives that target individuals without engaging network structure routinely underperform. Initiatives that engage network structure routinely outperform their predicted impact.

The three-degrees finding has been partially contested empirically (some replications have been mixed), but the broader insight — *behaviors and attitudes propagate through network structure rather than only through individual decision* — has held up under scrutiny. The seat does not require the exact "three" number; it requires the network-propagation framework.

### Move 3: Network Structure as Engineerable

From the Yale Human Nature Lab's experimental work. Network structures are not fixed natural phenomena; they can be deliberately modified, with predictable effects on what propagates through the network. The lab's experiments include creating artificial social networks (in field experiments in Honduras and elsewhere) with deliberately-designed structures and observing how the structural design affects health behaviors, cooperation rates, and information propagation.

**Operational deployment:** when MAGI analyzes a coordination problem, the network-engineering check asks — *what network structure currently exists, what structure would better support the desired propagation, and what intervention would shift from current to desired?* The answer often involves connecting nodes that are currently disconnected, or breaking connections that are currently propagating undesired content.

This is the operational counterpart of Move 2. Three-degrees-of-influence describes how networks behave; network engineering specifies how they can be deliberately altered to produce different propagation patterns.

### Move 4: The Dunbar Architecture as Biological Substrate

The substrate beneath the Wooster Code's central argument. Robin Dunbar's findings on cognitive limits to social relationships (~150-person stable groups) are not isolated empirical claims; they fit into Christakis's broader social-suite framework as the *cognitive constraint* on the social-network capacity humans evolved to maintain.

The architecture: humans evolved in groups of ~150 (Dunbar limit), organized by the social suite (the eight species-universals), with three-degrees-of-influence operating across the network. *Civilizations operate at scales that exceed the Dunbar limit by orders of magnitude*. The only mechanism humans have ever found for coordinating at civilizational scale is external coordination infrastructure — culture, tradition, institutions, shared narrative — that substitutes for the personal-knowledge coordination that operates naturally within Dunbar limits.

**Operational deployment:** when MAGI analyzes a coordination problem at scale, the Dunbar-architecture check asks — *is the proposed coordination operating within Dunbar scale (where personal-knowledge mechanisms work) or beyond it (where institutional infrastructure is required)?* Confusing the two produces predictable failures — Dunbar-scale solutions deployed at civilizational scale, or civilizational-scale solutions deployed at Dunbar scale.

The Wooster Code substrate (`the_wooster_code.md`) develops this architecture at length. Christakis's seat provides the biological foundation; the Wooster Code provides the civilizational-historical analysis of what happens when the foundation is ignored.

### Move 5: The Empirical-Discipline Disposition

The seat's methodological move. Christakis's work is grounded in actual empirical research — his own studies, the broader social-science findings, the historical record on plagues and social disruptions. This is operationally distinct from much social theory that proceeds from premises to conclusions without engaging empirical work.

The discipline: claims about human nature, social organization, and civilizational dynamics should be *checked against the empirical record* before deployment. The empirical record is uneven (some claims well-established, others contested, others outright wrong) and the discipline includes calibrating confidence to the evidence quality.

**Operational deployment:** when MAGI processes a claim about how humans behave, organize themselves, or respond to conditions, the empirical-discipline check asks — *what does the actual evidence say about this claim, and is the confidence level matched to the evidence quality?* The check protects against both overconfident pop-science claims and overconfident social-theory claims that proceed without empirical grounding.

## Calibration

The replication crisis in social science has affected some specific Christakis findings. The exact "three degrees" number has been contested empirically; some specific contagion effects have weaker effect sizes than originally reported; the network-engineering field experiments have produced mixed results across replications. The seat does not require all empirical specifics to remain canonical — it requires the *framework* (social suite, network propagation, Dunbar architecture, empirical-discipline disposition) to remain operationally useful, which it does.

The "evolutionary origins of a good society" framing in *Blueprint* has been critiqued by some evolutionary biologists as overstating the case for evolved cooperation and underweighting evolved competition, deception, and intergroup violence. The critiques are partially fair. The seat does not require defending every claim in *Blueprint*; it requires the social-suite framework as analytical apparatus for civilizational analysis grounded in evolutionary findings.

The empirical-discipline disposition can be misread as scientism. The seat does not require treating empirical research as the only legitimate source of knowledge; it requires *calibrating confidence to evidence quality* across all the analytical apparatus the brain deploys. Some questions have strong empirical answers; some have weak empirical answers; some have no available empirical answers and must be analyzed through other means. The discipline is to know which is which.

The seat's operational deployment can drift toward biological reductionism if the cultural specifications are not held alongside the species-universals. The discipline is to hold both — *humans are evolutionarily designed for the social suite AND cultures elaborate on the suite in radically different specifications*. Either alone produces partial analysis; together they produce the synthesis the brain requires.
