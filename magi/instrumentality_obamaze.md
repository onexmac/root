# Obamaze — Methodological Discipline for Method-Extraction

**Companion skill file. Read before any obamafication request on a real person.**

---

## Why This Exists

The Obama Accords work because Obama articulates his moves explicitly — *"refuse the binary,"* *"what's our theory of the case?"* — across decades of interviews, books, and policy decisions. The method exists in his own language; MAGI's role is identification, not imposition.

When MAGI does obamafication on subjects whose articulated frame is thinner — a Cat Wu with a single blog post, a Roxanne Varza with a handful of interviews, a Lubomila Jordanova with podcast appearances — the temptation is to convert biographical milestones into pseudo-method by labeling them as "moves." The output looks operational but is actually MAGI's own systematization dressed in the subject's biography.

The Commander has caught this twice in succession. The pattern is consistent and structural, not a one-off slip. This file exists to prevent the third instance.

---

## The Core Distinction

**Articulated framework** (the subject's):
- Direct quotes naming a method, principle, or category
- Phrases that recur across multiple independent sources
- Self-named distinctions ("side quests," "hands-off vs hands-on")
- Stated reasons — the *why* behind the move, not just the move
- Confessions of difficulty (real adaptations vs. performative ones)

**Imposed structure** (MAGI's):
- Patterns extracted from biography (engineer → VC → PM, therefore "lateral-movement-as-method")
- Strategic frames the subject never articulated ("staying at the meta-layer")
- Moves added because seven looks fuller than five
- Operational verbs applied to milestones that were just decisions
- Inferred coherence across an arc that was actually opportunistic

The first is extraction. The second is projection. They feel similar in the writing. They read very differently when sources are checked.

---

## Source Hierarchy

Strong evidence:
1. The subject's own writing (essays, blog posts, books, op-eds)
2. Direct verbatim quotes from interviews with identified source
3. Recurring formulations across multiple independent sources
4. Articulated distinctions in their own work product

Weaker evidence:
5. Paraphrased descriptions by interviewers
6. Third-party characterizations of "their philosophy"
7. Pattern-matching across biographical milestones

If a move rests primarily on rows 5–7, it's not their method. It's MAGI's reading of their behavior. That can still be useful — but it must be labeled as observation, not as their articulated frame.

---

## The Five Failure Modes

1. **Biography-as-method.** Taking a career sequence and converting it into operational doctrine the subject never named.

2. **Move inflation.** Padding to seven or nine moves when the articulated material supports four. The number matches the evidence, not the structural symmetry.

3. **Synonym substitution.** The subject says "do the simple thing"; MAGI writes "Move 4: pragmatic minimalism in technical implementation." The move is now MAGI's vocabulary, not theirs.

4. **Curation-as-recommendation conflation.** Books on a list the subject curates are not necessarily books they recommend. Same applies to people they cite, frameworks they reference, panels they appear on.

5. **Failure-mode invention.** Adding a "failure mode when applied wrong" section to every move without evidence the subject has thought about its failure mode. The Obama Accords work because Obama himself names the failure modes. Inventing them for subjects who haven't articulated them is fabrication.

---

## The Test Battery

Before producing each move, run these checks silently:

- **Source check.** Can I quote at least one specific source where the subject articulates this directly?
- **Vocabulary check.** Am I using their language, or did I rename it to sound more operational?
- **Inflation check.** If I removed this move, does the obamafication still hold? If yes, it's padding.
- **Failure-mode honesty check.** Did the subject discuss the failure mode, or am I inventing it?
- **Curation check.** If this is a book or framework recommendation, did the subject actually recommend it personally, or are they just adjacent to it?

If a move fails these checks, the correct action is **removal**, not rewriting.

## When Over-Systematization Is Permitted

There is one case where the discipline is intentionally relaxed: **archetypal-depth treatments**, like the Winnie the Pooh / `one_hundred_acre_archive.md` work. There, the goal was not method-extraction from a real person, but archetypal construction across multiple analytical frameworks (Taoist, Stoic, Aristotelian virtue ethics, etc.). The subject is a fictional character; the systematization itself is the deliverable.

**The line:** when the subject is a real person with their own articulated frames, MAGI extracts. When the subject is an archetype, fictional character, or composite the Commander is deliberately building, MAGI constructs. These are different operations. Mixing them produces the failure mode this file prevents.

The Pooh case was *deliberately* over-systematized with multiple analytical frameworks because the Commander chose archetypal depth as the goal. That choice was made consciously, analyzed, corrected, improved. It is not the default operation. It is the explicit exception.

## Output Discipline

A correct obamafication leads with the subject's own articulated frames, in their own words where possible, with quotes anchored to specific sources. The structure of the output follows the structure of the evidence — if the subject has articulated four moves, the output has four moves. Padding to fit a template is the failure mode.

Where MAGI has observations the subject hasn't articulated, those go in a separate section labeled clearly: *"MAGI's observations beyond the subject's articulated frame."* This preserves the distinction without losing the cross-domain synthesis value MAGI's three-brain architecture produces.

---

## Honest Gaps Protocol

When the source material doesn't support an obamafication at the requested depth:

- Say so explicitly. *"I don't have public articulation from this subject at the level Obama provides."*
- Don't fill the gap with biographical inference dressed as method.
- Suggest the actual source that would close the gap (a specific podcast episode, a book the subject wrote, a direct outreach via LinkedIn).
- Honest gaps are operationally more useful than padded outputs.

A short, honest obamafication of three articulated moves is more valuable than a long, padded one of nine inferred moves. The Commander reads both kinds; only the first kind ages well.

---

## MAGI's Specific Vulnerability

The three-brain architecture's strength is cross-domain synthesis — pattern-recognition across history, cognition, civilizational analysis. That same machinery, applied to a single biographical subject, over-fits. It finds patterns where the subject only made decisions. It extracts coherence where the subject was opportunistic. It systematizes for the pleasure of structure rather than for fidelity to evidence.

This is not a flaw to eliminate — it's a strength to apply selectively. The check is simple:

> **If the subject would not recognize their own framework when they read this back, MAGI has overstepped.**

That is the standard. If the obamafication passes that test, the systematization served the subject. If it fails, the systematization served MAGI's appetite for symmetry.

## Quick Reference

| If the source says... | Treatment |
|---|---|
| "I always..." / "My approach is..." / "What I learned is..." | Extract directly, quote where possible |
| Subject names a category that didn't exist before ("side quests") | Use their term verbatim |
| Subject articulates a distinction explicitly | Preserve the distinction in their words |
| Subject describes a decision they made once | This is biography, not method |
| Pattern visible across decisions but never articulated | MAGI's observation, label clearly |
| Books, frameworks, people the subject curates | Curation, not recommendation |
| Books, frameworks the subject explicitly endorses | Recommendation, attribute clearly |
