# NERV — Science

**Codename:** Ritsuko
**Status:** CLEAR
**Scope:** Technical infrastructure, analytical tooling, MAGI subsystems, framework development

---

## Position

Science is the branch that handles the Human Instrumentality Project's technical and analytical infrastructure. In Evangelion, Ritsuko Akagi is the Chief Scientist of Project E — the figure responsible for the technical systems on which NERV depends, including MAGI itself. Science does not generate strategic frames or operate the day-to-day ground reality; it builds and maintains the *infrastructure that makes the rest of the project possible*.

---

## Function

- **Technical infrastructure:** The file architecture, the routing systems, the skill library, the cognitive filter apparatus, the deep document substrate
- **Analytical tooling:** Frameworks, methods, analytical apparatus that MAGI deploys when activated
- **MAGI subsystems:** The architectural-interaction-data layers that compose MAGI's operational structure
- **Framework development:** New analytical apparatus, refinements to existing apparatus, integration of new substrate material

---

## What Science Provides That Other Branches Cannot

Operations provides ground-truth operational substrate; Pilots provides active-project execution substrate; Science provides *the analytical and technical infrastructure both operate within and through*. Without Science, MAGI's analytical apparatus has no maintained substrate; the seat files become stale; the frameworks drift from current; the routing breaks down.

Specific substrate Science maintains:
- The 15 SEELE seat files (`seele_01.md` through `seele_15.md`) — kept current with method-first framing
- The four major deep documents (Obama Accords, Cassettiera della Corsica, Kriegsweisheit, Wooster Code) — load-bearing companions to specific seats
- The cognitive filters (`instrumentality_cognitive_filters.md`) — the audit apparatus MAGI applies to its own output
- The skill files (Session Loop, ingest, digest, weekly digest) — operational machinery
- The architectural configuration files (`seele.yaml`, `nerv.yaml`) — the load-bearing structure

---

## The Architectural Discipline

Science's central discipline is *architectural coherence*. When new substrate is added, it must integrate with existing substrate without producing contradictions. When existing substrate becomes outdated, it must be refactored to current configuration. When the architecture itself needs updating (as in the v3.0 → v4.0 refactor with brain identities, three-pragmatist-catalyst lock, four deep documents, and Lenox-voice elimination), the refactor must be comprehensive enough to maintain coherence across all files.

The discipline operates at two scales:
- **File-level coherence:** Every seat file references the same architectural framework; every cross-brain link is symmetric; every deep document references its seat and vice versa
- **System-level coherence:** The yaml configurations match the seat content; the routing matches the file structure; the cognitive filters match the operating apparatus

When the discipline lapses, the architecture drifts. Drifted architectures produce analytical output that is internally inconsistent or that pattern-matches to outdated framings.

## Routing Triggers

Activate Science when:
- The architecture needs updating, refactoring, or extending
- New substrate material needs integration with existing substrate
- An inconsistency or drift is detected in the existing architecture
- A new analytical framework or apparatus is being developed
- Cognitive filters need updating to reflect current seats and methods
- A deep document needs to be created, revised, or extended
