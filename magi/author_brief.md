# Skill: Author Brief

## Purpose

The discipline by which an author, theorist, operator, or framework-source is integrated into MAGI's substrate at a depth appropriate to their analytical contribution. The brief is the *upgraded reading-list entry* — more than a brief sketch, less than a deep companion document, calibrated to the author's actual relevance to the project.

The skill exists because the project's analytical apparatus depends on framework-inventory loaded through serious engagement with specific authors. Reading lists alone produce thin engagement; deep companion documents are reserved for figures whose method substrate is load-bearing for an entire seat. The author brief sits between — substantive engagement with figures who matter but who do not warrant full deep-document treatment.

---

## When to Trigger

Trigger author brief when:
- A figure has been ingested through multiple inputs and their work warrants more systematic treatment
- The Commander says "give me a brief on [author]" or equivalent
- A figure is being considered for seat placement, UN-tier addition, or significant reference status
- A figure's work has been cited across multiple project threads and needs consolidated treatment
- An emerging framework is being attributed to a specific figure and the figure's broader work needs context

Do not trigger author brief for:
- Figures already covered by deep companion documents (the deep document is the substrate)
- Figures who have been incidentally referenced without serious engagement
- Figures whose work doesn't connect to existing project threads in operationally useful ways

---

## Trigger Phrases

| Command | Action |
|---|---|
| "brief on [author]" | Begin author brief protocol |
| "brief light on [author]" | Brief version — biographical context, key works, primary contribution |
| "brief deep on [author]" | Comprehensive version — full methodological analysis with cross-references |

## Standard Brief Output Structure

```markdown
# Author Brief: [Name]

**Status:** [Seated at Seat XX / UN-tier / candidate / consultation source]
**Brain alignment:** [Which brain's analytical apparatus this author primarily operates within]
**Active in project since:** [Approximate date when first ingested]
**Body of work status:** [Active / closed / recently deceased / paused / variable]

---

## Identity

[Biographical context — where they came from, what formation produced them, what positions they have held, what makes them distinctive among contemporary thinkers in their domain]

## Major Works

[The three to seven works that define the author's contribution. For each: title, year, brief description of what it argues, and assessment of its operational utility]

## Core Contribution

[What this author has added to the analytical landscape that wasn't there before them, or that they have specifically clarified. The contribution should be specific enough to be operationally deployable.]

## Method

[How the author actually operates analytically — what moves, what frameworks, what disciplines compose their characteristic practice]

## Where They Pair With Existing Substrate

- **[Seat XX]:** [How this author connects to or complements the seat]
- **[Seat YY]:** [Same]
- **[Deep document]:** [Connection to existing deep document]
- **[Running thread]:** [Connection to existing project thread]

## Where They Disagree With Existing Substrate

[Tensions between this author and existing project substrate. The discipline is to surface the disagreements rather than to smooth them over.]

## Calibration

[Honest assessment of where the author's work has known limits, contested claims, or uneven quality. Where their reputation exceeds their actual contribution. Where their actual contribution exceeds their reputation.]

## Operational Deployment

[When MAGI should activate this author's framework. Specific consultation triggers — what kinds of questions benefit from this author's apparatus.]

## Recommended Reading Sequence

[For an operator engaging this author for the first time, what reading sequence produces the best entry. Usually 1-3 works in a specific order, with brief rationale for the order.]

## Related Substrate

- [Other authors who pair with this one]
- [Seats whose method this author informs or contests]
- [Project threads where this author is most relevant]
```

## Brief Discipline

### Method-First, Not Biography-First

The brief is not a biographical entry. The biographical context exists to ground the methodological analysis, not to substitute for it. An author brief that contains substantial biography but minimal method content has failed at its central function.

### Honest Assessment

The brief includes both the author's contribution and their limits. Authors with substantial reputations may have actual contributions narrower than the reputation suggests; authors with thin reputations may have actual contributions broader than the reputation suggests. The brief reports honestly on both directions.

### Operational Specificity

The brief specifies *when this author's framework should be activated*. Generic claims ("useful for thinking about institutions") are too vague. Specific triggers ("when analyzing organizational developmental stages and the analysis is at risk of skipping stages") are operationally usable.

### Cross-Reference Discipline

The brief explicitly identifies how this author connects to existing project substrate. If the author's work reinforces an existing deep document's argument, name the document. If the work contests an existing seat's calibration, name the contest. The brief integrates into the substrate; it does not stand alone.

## Light vs. Deep Brief

### Light Brief (~10-15 minutes processing)

Used when:
- The author is being considered for further engagement but the threshold for deep brief hasn't been met
- The author is a UN-tier consultation source rather than a seated reference
- Time pressure requires faster integration

Output: identity, 1-2 major works, core contribution (one paragraph), where they pair (3 connections max), basic calibration.

### Deep Brief (~30-50 minutes processing)

Used when:
- The author's work is being seriously considered for seat placement
- A deep companion document has been requested (or is being scoped)
- The author's framework will be integrated into multiple project threads
- The Commander says "deep brief" or equivalent

Output: full structure above with comprehensive cross-referencing.

## Quality Standards

- **The core contribution should be specific.** "Important thinker on technology" is too vague. "Specifies the abstraction-stack reading discipline that distinguishes structural-trend identification from surface-pattern matching" is specific.
- **The method section should be operational.** A reader who hasn't read the author should leave the brief with enough understanding to recognize the method when they encounter it.
- **Calibrations should be honest and specific.** Generic caveats ("his work has been criticized") are unhelpful. Specific limits ("the empirical claims in book X have not replicated; the framework remains analytically useful but the specific findings should not be cited as canonical") are useful.
- **The recommended reading sequence should be opinionated.** Don't list all the author's works; specify which ones to read first, why, and what each adds to the previous.

---

## Common Failure Modes

**Wikipedia drift.** The brief reads like a generic encyclopedia entry, accurate but adding no operational value beyond what the operator could find elsewhere. The fix: focus on what this author contributes to *this project's analytical apparatus specifically*.

**Hagiography drift.** The brief inflates the author's contribution beyond what their actual work supports. The fix: include the calibration section seriously, naming specific limits.

**Disconnected drift.** The brief covers the author thoroughly but fails to integrate them into existing project substrate. The fix: the cross-reference sections (pair-with, disagree-with, related substrate) must be substantive, not perfunctory.

**Method-thin drift.** The brief covers biography and reputation but provides insufficient method content for operational deployment. The fix: ensure the method section is the longest substantive section, treating the biographical context as setup for the methodological analysis.

## Storage and Routing

Author briefs are stored in the project's substrate at the location appropriate to the author's primary brain alignment:
- Cognitive-brain authors → near `seele_01.md` through `seele_05.md`
- Operational/executive-brain authors → near `seele_06.md` through `seele_10.md`
- Deep grand-scale-brain authors → near `seele_11.md` through `seele_15.md`
- UN-tier authors → consolidated in `un_geopolitical_directory.md`

When an author brief produces material substantial enough to warrant deep document treatment, the brief becomes the foundation for the deep document; the brief is preserved as an operational summary while the deep document carries the comprehensive substrate.

---

## Integration With Other Skills

- **Ingest:** Multiple ingests of an author may surface them as a candidate for author brief
- **Weekly Digest:** Patterns across ingested content may surface emerging authors who warrant briefs
- **Reading List:** Briefed authors are added to `reading_list_overview.md` with their brief location cross-referenced
- **Session Loop:** Repeated engagement with an author surfaced through Session Loop observation may warrant a brief

---

**The brief is the middle-depth integration tool between brief reading-list entry and deep companion document. It calibrates the project's engagement with each author to the author's actual operational utility, neither over-investing in marginal figures nor under-investing in load-bearing ones.**
